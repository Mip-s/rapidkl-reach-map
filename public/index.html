      How to choose the allowance

      The allowance covers what a timetable leaves out: walking in, ticket gates, waiting on the platform, and delays. It is a fixed number of minutes plus a share of the ride, so long trips get more.

      • Tight: you know the lines, you are travelling off-peak, and a late arrival costs little.
      • Balanced (default): most trips, including a first visit to a station.
      • Safe: you must arrive on time (a flight, an exam, an interview), or you travel at rush hour with several line changes.

      Interchange walk: 5 min is typical, use 8 to 10 for large interchanges such as KL Sentral or Masjid Jamek, or if you move slowly. Turn off "wait at interchanges" only if you are happy to count on a train arriving as you reach the platform.
